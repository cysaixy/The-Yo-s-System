Celebrating 2 Years of Excellence

Great Food.
Warm Company.

Conveniently stationed at Poblacion, Baggao, The~Yo's Resto Bar brings a curated food and dining experience straight to the community. We focus on giving premium, satisfying local and classic meals without a heavy price tag.

From regional favorites like our regional Pancit Batil Patung to overloaded snacks and top-tier coffee and matcha series, our doors are welcoming for friend groups, study breaks, and large special celebrations.
