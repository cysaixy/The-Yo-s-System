Our approach

Fewer ingredients.
More attention.

The-Yo's started with one idea: cook a short, changing menu extremely well instead of a long one adequately. We work with what's in season, keep plates simple, and let ingredients do the talking.

The dining room is quiet by design — low counters, natural light, a kitchen you can see into. It's a space meant for conversation, not spectacle.
